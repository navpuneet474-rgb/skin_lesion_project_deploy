    ABCDE Early Warning Signs

    Apply the ABCDE rule during monthly self-examinations to identify potential melanoma early.

    A
    Asymmetry
    One half unlike the other half
    B
    Border
    Irregular, ragged or blurred edges
    C
    Color
    Multiple or uneven shades
    D
    Diameter
    Greater than 6 mm across
    E
    Evolving
    Changes in size, shape or color
    Photo Guidelines
    • 1Use bright, even lighting — avoid shadows and flash glare.
    • 2Keep the camera steady and focused on the lesion.
    • 3Capture the lesion with a small margin of surrounding skin.
    • 4Avoid blurry, distant, or low-resolution photographs.
    • 5Upload JPG or PNG, then click "Analyze Lesion".
    Skin Protection
    • 1Apply broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30–50+) daily.
    • 2Avoid direct sun between 10 AM and 4 PM.
    • 3Do not use tanning lamps or artificial UV sources.
    • 4Wear protective clothing, hat, and sunglasses.
    • 5Reapply sunscreen every 2–3 hours when outdoors.

    Early Detection Saves Lives
    Early detection significantly increases survival rates in melanoma. Regular self-examinations and early professional consultation are critical.
